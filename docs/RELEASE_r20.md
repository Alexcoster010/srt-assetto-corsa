Requested race-game mechanic, not measured LC0 tire degradation. Each tire now accumulates wear continuously from distance traveled, core temperature, normal load and absolute longitudinal slip. Lateral and longitudinal force multipliers are equal for each tire, but may differ between tires. Cooling does not restore accumulated wear. No physical flat-spot geometry is added.

Nominal target: 100% fresh to 70% after 15 track lengths at 60 C, static load and slip ratio at or below 0.15. This is an analytical reference, not a measured typical-race calibration. Cornering, aero load, heat and sliding can shorten the stint. Smooth/cool driving can extend it. Grip has the existing 60% minimum. Finishing lap 15 does not force a particular grip value.

Formula per tire: wear increment = 0.02 * distance / track length * temperature factor * load factor * slip factor. Temperature factor = clamp(1 + 0.01*(core C - 60), 0.75, 1.8). Load factor = clamp(normal load/static wheel load, 0, 3)^1.2. Static load derives from 280.5 kg and front share 0.4774245473. Slip factor = 1 + 3*clamp((abs(slip ratio)-0.15)/0.85, 0, 1). Thus severe lockups or wheelspin can multiply wear by four. No wear below 5 km/h or with load below 20 N; airborne wheels do not wear. Stationary burnout wear is not modeled. Forces approach the accumulated-wear target with a 0.5-second smoothing time constant.

Track length comes from CSP getSim().trackLengthM (AI spline length). Missing/invalid length uses a clearly flagged 1000 m fallback, so the 15-lap target does not apply on such tracks. Use correctly measured AI splines for race testing. Driving extra distance, including moving in the pits, also consumes tire life.

Pit-reset behavior unchanged: native four-tire virtual-distance reset detected while stationary in your own pit box, or 10 uninterrupted seconds below 0.5 km/h in your own pit box, restores all four virtual tires. The timed option works even without selecting native tire service. Leaving the box interrupts the timer. Teleport alone retains wear. A decreasing session lap counter resets the stint; script reload/reconnect may also reset it. This is not server-authoritative. All racers need matching files and compatible CSP. AI pit strategy is not automated.
