# SRT r13 revised — 600 Nm brakes

Re-released 2026-09-27 after both drivers reported continued lockup with the initial800Nm r13. Maximum brake torque is now600Nm, a further25% reduction. Front share remains75%. Tire grip, FFB, engine, gearing and all other physics are unchanged.

**Download the ZIP ending `_600Nm.zip`.** UI version0.13.1 identifies this revised r13. Reinstall even if you already have r13, and restart the driving session. Full car for original PC Assetto Corsa; requires CSP0.2.11. Back up your existing srt27_prototype folder first.

Validation: brake configuration and package/source consistency checked; driver validation pending. No new stopping-distance or braking-g result is claimed. Reduced torque is intended to provide more pedal travel before lockup, not to eliminate lockup at all pedal pressures.

Optional track ZIPs and all r12 model limitations remain unchanged. Historical notes describe earlier settings; this note supersedes their brake torque. No third-party asset rights are granted. For rollback, public r12 remains available but has stronger1142Nm brakes.
